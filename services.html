Workspace deployment

Set up once. Used every day.

We connect the assistant you already pay for, Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot, to your email, calendar, files and CRM. Then we find the tasks eating your week and turn each one into a saved skill or a scheduled job.

  • Email, calendar, files and CRM connected
  • Repeat tasks saved as one-click skills
  • Reports and follow-ups on a schedule
  • A working session with your team
Managed agents

Work that hands itself off.

Each agent owns one step. One takes the request in, one does the work, one checks it before anything leaves. We build the chain, watch it every day, and fix a step the moment it drifts.

  • One agent per step, each with its own permissions
  • A person signs off before anything goes out
  • Weekly scorecard of runs, saves and misses
  • Changes handled by us, not you
Managed AI spend

Pay for what gets used.

Most teams pay for seats nobody opens and starve the work that matters. We right-size the plans, set a hard ceiling per person and per workflow, and test every automated step against the cheapest tool that can do it.

  • Seat and plan audit
  • Hard monthly caps per user and workflow
  • Monthly usage report
  • 12-month cost test on every step
Security + governance

Nothing moves without you.

Every agent gets the smallest set of permissions its job needs. Anything that sends, spends or publishes waits for your approval, and every account stays in your name.

  • Written AI use policy for your team
  • Permissions set per action, not per tool
  • Approval on every send, spend or publish
  • You own every account and file
