AI spend that scales on purpose.Every dollar tied to a job.

Most companies buy AI one seat and one experiment at a time, with no clear view of cost or return. I set it up the other way: one account you own, with clear limits.

01

You own the account

Your account, billing, keys and data. I work inside it with the least access the job needs, and can be removed in minutes.

02

Cheapest path that does the job

Each task gets a 12-month cost test against the tools you already pay for. If a rule or schedule can do it, that's what gets built. AI handles only the steps that need judgment.

03

The right size model for each step

Simple checks run on the smallest tier, writing on the middle tier, and the top tier only where it's needed.

04

Hard ceilings, not surprises

Monthly spend limits and a hard budget on every run. Non-urgent work is batched overnight at a lower rate.

05

One scorecard

A weekly view of cost, output and hours saved, measured against three goals we set at the start.
